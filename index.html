Meet Your Doctor

Dr. Saiful Islam

BDS MDS (Anatomy) MS MPH BCS

Dr. Saiful Islam is a distinguished dental specialist and Head of Dental Anatomy at Dhaka Medical College & Hospital. With advanced degrees in dental surgery and public health, he brings both clinical excellence and academic depth to his practice.

His private chamber at Saiful's Dental Care in Mirpur offers accessible, high-quality care to patients across Dhaka. Dr. Islam is known for his patient-first approach — explaining every step clearly and making dental visits comfortable.

  • Professor & Head of Department — Dhaka Medical College & Hospital
  • Specialist in Dental Anatomy, Oral Pathology & Periodontics
  • Former D.C.S. (Dental Chief Surgeon), Dhaka
  • Experienced in both emergency and complex dental cases
  • Over 10 years of clinical and academic service in Bangladesh
